Privacy Policy

This policy explains how FutureMakers handles information when you visit our site, contact us, purchase, or participate in a project.

Effective and last updated: August 18, 2026

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to futuremakers.dev, FutureMakers forms and communications, and services that link to this policy. A third-party platform you choose to use is governed by its own privacy policy.

2. Information we collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect:

  • Contact information: name, email address, telephone number, business name, and social or website links.
  • Transaction information: product, price, purchase date, billing status, and limited payment metadata. Payment-card details are collected and processed by our payment processor rather than stored directly by FutureMakers.
  • Project information: onboarding answers, goals, preferences, messages, meeting notes or recordings when disclosed, source files, photos, voice or video assets, brand materials, account identifiers, approvals, and deliverables.
  • Technical information: IP address, browser and device data, referral source, pages viewed, approximate location, cookie identifiers, and site interactions.
  • Communications: support requests, feedback, survey answers, and records of your correspondence with us.

3. How we use information

We use information to operate the site; respond to inquiries; verify and process purchases; onboard and deliver services; personalize project output; communicate about projects, billing, security, and policy changes; maintain records; prevent fraud or abuse; improve our offers and workflows; enforce agreements; comply with law; and send marketing when permitted. You can unsubscribe from marketing emails using the link provided, although transactional messages may continue.

4. How we share information

We do not sell personal information for money. We may disclose information to payment processors, hosting and analytics providers, email and scheduling services, cloud storage, contractors, professional advisers, and AI or creative platforms when reasonably needed to operate or deliver the service. These providers process information under their own terms or commitments.

We may also disclose information when required by law; to protect rights, safety, and security; to investigate fraud or abuse; with your direction or consent; or in connection with a merger, financing, acquisition, reorganization, or transfer of all or part of the business.

5. AI tools and project materials

Some services use AI-assisted tools. Project information submitted to those tools may be processed by third-party providers. We seek to limit submissions to what is reasonably needed, but you should not send passwords, government identifiers, financial-account numbers, health data, confidential third-party records, or other highly sensitive information unless we expressly agree in writing on an approved method.

6. Cookies and analytics

We and our providers may use necessary cookies and similar technologies for site functionality, security, checkout, preferences, analytics, and marketing measurement. Browser controls can limit cookies, but some functions may not work correctly. Where required, we will request consent before using nonessential cookies.

7. Data retention

We retain information only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described here, including delivery, support, business records, tax and accounting obligations, dispute resolution, security, and enforcement. Retention periods vary by the type of information and legal requirements. We may delete or de-identify information when it is no longer needed.

8. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards appropriate to the nature of the information. No internet transmission, storage system, or service can be guaranteed completely secure. You are responsible for sending materials through appropriate channels and protecting credentials associated with your accounts.

9. Your choices and privacy requests

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, portability, or information about certain disclosures, and to appeal a decision. You may also opt out of marketing communications. To submit a request, email support@futuremakers.dev with the subject “Privacy Request.” We may verify your identity and may retain information where legally permitted or required.

We will not discriminate against you for exercising an applicable privacy right. Authorized-agent requests may require proof of authorization and identity verification.

10. Children

FutureMakers services are intended for adults and are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from a child under 13. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us so we can investigate and delete it as appropriate.

11. International use

FutureMakers operates from the United States. If you access the service from another country, information may be transferred to and processed in the United States or other countries where our providers operate, which may have different privacy laws.

12. External links

Our site may link to third-party websites and platforms. We are not responsible for their privacy, security, content, or practices. Review their policies before providing information.

13. Changes

We may update this policy by posting a revised version and effective date. If a change materially affects how we use previously collected information, we will provide additional notice where required.

14. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email support@futuremakers.dev with the subject “Privacy Request.”
