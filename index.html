No. The process is designed around AI-assisted tools such as Grok, Lovable, and Stripe. You will still make business decisions and follow instructions, but you do not need to begin as a programmer.

No honest product can guarantee that. The Blueprint helps you complete the infrastructure and thinking required to test an offer. Sales depend on demand, positioning, execution, distribution, and continued improvement.

No. The sensible use of this process is to build and test alongside your current responsibilities. The goal is to increase your options—not pressure you into reckless decisions.

You should have a defined customer problem, a shaped offer, a focused product page, and a live payment path, along with a clearer plan for testing the offer with real people.
